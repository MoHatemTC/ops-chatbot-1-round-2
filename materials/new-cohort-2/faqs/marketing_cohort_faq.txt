MARKETING COHORT — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

Document Type: FAQ
Cohort: Marketing
Purpose: Approved operational reference material for learners in the Marketing cohort.

============================================================
1. GENERAL COHORT INFORMATION
============================================================

Q1. What is the Marketing cohort?
A: The Marketing cohort is a training program focused on practical marketing skills, campaign planning, audience understanding, content strategy, digital channels, analytics, teamwork, and project delivery. Learners are expected to participate actively in sessions, complete assigned tasks, and apply the concepts in practical activities.

Q2. Who should use this FAQ?
A: This FAQ is intended for learners enrolled in the Marketing cohort. It contains common operational information about attendance, tasks, sessions, communication, submissions, teamwork, and learner support.

Q3. Where should I check for official cohort updates?
A: Learners should check the official cohort communication channels and the learning platform regularly. Important updates may include session announcements, deadline changes, task instructions, event reminders, or administrative notices.

Q4. Can information from another cohort be applied to the Marketing cohort?
A: No. Learners should follow only information published for the Marketing cohort. Schedules, deadlines, assignments, requirements, and announcements may differ between cohorts.

============================================================
2. SESSIONS AND ATTENDANCE
============================================================

Q5. How often are Marketing cohort sessions held?
A: Sessions are held according to the official Marketing cohort schedule. Learners should rely on the latest published schedule rather than assuming that sessions occur on the same day every week.

Q6. What should I do before a session?
A: Review any required pre-session material, prepare questions, complete assigned preparation tasks, and make sure you have access to the required platform or meeting link.

Q7. What should I do if I cannot attend a session?
A: Notify the appropriate cohort contact as early as possible when required, then review any available session material and complete missed work. Repeated absences may affect progress in the program.

Q8. Does joining late count as attendance?
A: Attendance rules may consider significant lateness separately from full attendance. Learners should join sessions on time and remain present for the expected duration.

Q9. Will sessions be recorded?
A: Recording availability depends on the specific session and program policy. Learners should not depend on recordings as a substitute for attending live sessions unless the cohort team explicitly confirms otherwise.

Q10. What happens if a session is rescheduled?
A: The new date or time will be communicated through an official channel. Learners should follow the most recent announcement.

============================================================
3. TASKS AND DEADLINES
============================================================

Q11. Where can I find my Marketing tasks?
A: Tasks are shared through the official learning platform, cohort workspace, or communication channel specified by the program team. Always use the latest published task instructions.

Q12. How should I submit an assignment?
A: Follow the submission method specified in the assignment instructions. This may include submitting a document, presentation, campaign plan, link, spreadsheet, or another required deliverable.

Q13. What happens if I miss a deadline?
A: Late submissions may be treated according to the cohort's task policy. If you expect to miss a deadline because of a genuine issue, communicate early rather than waiting until after the deadline.

Q14. Can I edit my work after submitting it?
A: This depends on the submission platform and assignment rules. If editing is allowed, make sure the final version is submitted before the deadline. If it is not allowed, contact the responsible team member for guidance.

Q15. How do I know that my submission was received?
A: Check for a confirmation message, status change, uploaded file, or submission record on the platform. If the platform does not show confirmation, verify with the cohort team before the deadline.

Q16. What should I do if the submission platform has a technical problem?
A: Save evidence of the issue, such as a screenshot and the time it occurred, then report the problem through the approved support channel. Keep your completed work ready so it can be submitted when the issue is resolved.

============================================================
4. MARKETING PROJECTS
============================================================

Q17. What type of projects might I work on?
A: Marketing projects may involve market research, customer personas, competitor analysis, campaign planning, content calendars, social media strategy, branding, channel selection, performance analysis, or presenting a marketing proposal.

Q18. Are projects individual or team-based?
A: The format depends on the assigned activity. Some tasks may be individual, while larger projects may require collaboration with a team.

Q19. How should a team divide project work?
A: Teams should define clear responsibilities, deadlines, ownership, and communication methods. Common roles may include research, strategy, content, design coordination, analytics, presentation preparation, and project organization.

Q20. What should we do if a team member is inactive?
A: Contact the team member first and clarify the expected responsibility. If the problem continues and affects the project, document the issue and notify the appropriate mentor or Operations contact.

Q21. Can we change the project topic after starting?
A: Do not make a major project change without checking the assignment requirements. If approval is required, ask before investing significant time in the new direction.

============================================================
5. MARKETING TOOLS AND MATERIALS
============================================================

Q22. Do I need paid marketing tools?
A: Learners should not purchase paid tools unless the program explicitly requires them. Free plans, trial versions, provided accounts, or alternative tools should normally be used when they meet the task requirements.

Q23. Which platforms might be used during the cohort?
A: Depending on the activity, learners may work with presentation tools, spreadsheets, social media platforms, analytics dashboards, collaboration tools, design tools, survey tools, or campaign-planning resources.

Q24. What should I do if I cannot access a required tool?
A: First check your account, invitation, internet connection, and the provided setup instructions. If access still fails, report the exact error and identify the tool you are trying to use.

Q25. Can I use AI tools for Marketing assignments?
A: AI tools may be used only when allowed by the assignment or cohort policy. Learners remain responsible for checking accuracy, originality, quality, and compliance with the task requirements.

Q26. Can I copy marketing content generated by AI directly into my project?
A: Learners should review, verify, and adapt any generated content before using it. Unchecked output may contain inaccurate claims, unsuitable tone, weak positioning, or information that does not match the project.

============================================================
6. FEEDBACK AND ASSESSMENT
============================================================

Q27. How will my Marketing work be evaluated?
A: Evaluation depends on the assignment, but common factors may include understanding of the problem, quality of research, strategic reasoning, audience relevance, clarity, execution, teamwork, and presentation quality.

Q28. What should I do if I do not understand my feedback?
A: Review the feedback carefully and identify the specific part that is unclear. Then ask a focused question about the expected improvement.

Q29. Can I request feedback before the final submission?
A: If draft feedback is supported for that assignment, learners may ask for it through the approved mentor or review process. Draft review is not guaranteed unless the task instructions allow it.

Q30. Will every task receive a grade?
A: Not necessarily. Some activities may be practice exercises, participation tasks, checkpoints, or feedback-based assignments rather than formally graded work.

============================================================
7. COMMUNICATION AND SUPPORT
============================================================

Q31. Who should I contact if I have an operational question?
A: Use the approved cohort support channel or the Operations support process. Operational questions include schedules, attendance, deadlines, access, submissions, and program procedures.

Q32. What should I do if the chatbot cannot answer my question?
A: If the answer is not available in the approved Marketing cohort materials, the question should be referred to the Operations team rather than answered using unsupported information.

Q33. What information should I include when reporting a problem?
A: Include a short description of the problem, what you were trying to do, what happened, any error message, and relevant evidence such as a screenshot when appropriate. Do not share passwords, access tokens, or other sensitive credentials.

Q34. How should I ask a question to get a useful answer?
A: Give enough context to identify the issue. For example, mention the task, session, tool, or process you are asking about. You do not need to use exact keywords, but a clear question usually makes support faster.

Q35. Can I ask questions unrelated to the Marketing cohort?
A: The cohort support assistant is intended to answer operational questions covered by approved cohort materials. Questions outside those materials may require a human or another appropriate resource.

============================================================
8. PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT
============================================================

Q36. What behavior is expected during the cohort?
A: Learners are expected to communicate respectfully, participate professionally, meet commitments, respect teammates, follow program instructions, and contribute constructively to group activities.

Q37. Can I share cohort materials publicly?
A: Do not publish or redistribute internal materials unless the program explicitly permits it. Some presentations, documents, links, datasets, or recordings may be intended only for enrolled learners.

Q38. How should disagreements inside a project team be handled?
A: Discuss the issue respectfully, focus on the project requirements, clarify responsibilities, and try to reach a practical solution. If the disagreement blocks progress, request mentor or Operations support.

Q39. What should I do if I receive inappropriate communication?
A: Preserve relevant evidence and report the issue through the approved program support or escalation channel.

============================================================
9. PROGRESS AND COMPLETION
============================================================

Q40. How can I stay on track in the Marketing cohort?
A: Check announcements regularly, attend sessions, track deadlines, complete tasks incrementally, keep project files organized, communicate with teammates, and ask for help before a blocker becomes urgent.

Q41. What if I fall behind?
A: Review your incomplete requirements, prioritize the nearest deadlines, and communicate early with the appropriate support person. Do not assume that missed work can automatically be submitted later.

Q42. How do I know whether I completed the cohort successfully?
A: Completion requirements are determined by the official program rules. These may include attendance, required tasks, project delivery, assessments, or other cohort-specific requirements.

Q43. Will I receive a certificate?
A: Certificate eligibility depends on the official program completion criteria. Learners should follow the cohort's published requirements and announcements regarding certificates.

============================================================
10. DATA, PRIVACY, AND SAFE USE
============================================================

Q44. Should I share my password with a mentor or teammate when I have an account problem?
A: No. Never share passwords, authentication codes, access tokens, or other secret credentials. Support staff can help diagnose access issues without asking you to reveal your password.

Q45. Can I use real customer data in a Marketing project?
A: Use only data that the project permits you to use. Do not include private, confidential, or personally identifiable customer information unless the program has explicitly authorized its use and provided the appropriate safeguards.

Q46. What should I do before publishing a campaign example?
A: Confirm that the content does not expose private learner information, confidential project information, restricted assets, or material that the program does not permit you to publish.

============================================================
11. QUICK SUPPORT GUIDE
============================================================

For a schedule question:
Check the latest official Marketing cohort schedule.

For a task question:
Check the latest assignment instructions and submission requirements.

For a deadline issue:
Contact the approved support channel as early as possible.
